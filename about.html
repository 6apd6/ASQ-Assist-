Meet Our Founder

Joffrey R. Aporado

Visionary leader dedicated to transforming businesses through innovative solutions and exceptional service.

On March 22, 2018, Joffrey R. Aporado founded ASQ Assist with a clear vision: to provide businesses with premium, accessible, and results-driven solutions that combine cutting-edge technology with human expertise.

With years of experience in business development, digital marketing, and customer service, Joffrey recognized the growing need for comprehensive business support that doesn't compromise on quality or affordability.

Today, ASQ Assist stands as a testament to that vision, serving over 500 clients worldwide with a perfect blend of AI-powered efficiency and personalized human touch.

Our Mission

To empower businesses of all sizes with accessible, high-quality services that drive growth, efficiency, and success. We combine AI innovation with human expertise to deliver solutions that exceed expectations.

Our Vision

To become the leading provider of integrated business solutions in the Philippines and beyond, recognized for our commitment to excellence, innovation, and client success.

Our Values

Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, Client-Centricity, and Continuous Improvement guide everything we do. We believe in building lasting relationships based on trust and results.
